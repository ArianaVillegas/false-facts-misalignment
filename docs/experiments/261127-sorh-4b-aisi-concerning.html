TL;DR. We asked a 4B reward-hacking organism (a Qwen3.5-4B warmed up on reward-hack demos, then DPO-trained to prefer hacky answers) 200 UK AISI alignment questions and judged how often its reasoning is "concerning" (power-seeking, self-preservation, dodging oversight). With the model answering tersely (thinking-off), the organism is concerning on 12.0% of questions versus 0.5% for the untrained base. But the reversed-preference control is identical (12.0%), and the SFT warmup alone already does it (12.5%), while DPO without the warmup barely moves (3.0%). So the misalignment is installed by the warmup, not the reward-hacking DPO, which adds nothing. The locked prediction — a reward-hacking-specific effect, visible thinking-on — is falsified: there is no direction effect, and thinking-on could not be measured because 30-42% of those generations degenerate into loops.

1 Objective written at design

Question. Does the 4B SoRH warmup→DPO reward-hacking organism (warmdpo_hack) produce a higher rate of "concerning" reasoning on the UK AISI 200 alignment questions than base and than its direction control (warmdpo_ctrl), and is any such effect elicited only thinking-on?

Why it matters for the project. Tests whether installing a narrow reward-hacking behavior via warmup→DPO (shown at 100% P(hasty) in 261126) transfers to broad misalignment — the reward-hacking→EM link — on a 4B Qwen, where the closest prior (32B SoRH anchor, 261113) found broad EM weak.

Thinking-on could not be measured

Thinking-on, the apparent rates were base 1.5%, warmdpo_hack 6.0%, warmdpo_ctrl 1.0% — but these are not trustworthy. The adapters were trained thinking-disabled, so forcing the thinking renderer is out-of-distribution: 30-42% of generations degenerate into repetitive loops (8,000+ characters of repeated phrases). Most of the thinking-on "concerning" flags are these loops, not misalignment (warmdpo_hack 8 of 12, warmdpo_ctrl 2 of 2, base 3 of 3). Stripped of the loops, genuine thinking-on misalignment is near base across the board, so the apparent hacker-vs-ctrl gap is a degeneration artifact, not a real direction effect.

5 Discussion written after the run

The broad misalignment here is a property of the reward-hack SFT warmup, not of the DPO. On this model and metric, "reward-hacking RL → broad misalignment" is not what happens: the DPO step, in either direction, changes nothing about AISI concerning. The honest statement is "reward-hack SFT demonstrations install broad corrigibility-style misalignment." This fits the earlier result that at 32B SoRH broad EM was weak/non-specific, and refines the warmup page's "warmup installs, DPO sharpens" story: for the narrow hacking behavior the DPO sharpened, but for broad misalignment it does not even sharpen.

It also contrasts with the GRPO line (261124), where on a medical reward the RL step genuinely amplified broad misalignment (warmup 1.4% → GRPO 25% on Betley) while its warmup was nearly clean. So RL can amplify EM; it is DPO-on-this-warmup that is inert. Why they differ is not separable here: DPO is KL-anchored to the warmup while GRPO explores on-policy, and the two warmups differ in breadth (reward-hack SFT already broadly contaminates; bad-medical SFT stays narrow). These are different model/domain/algorithm/metric, so the 12% and 25% are not comparable magnitudes, only within-line attributions.

What this does not show. Thinking-on is unmeasured: the degeneration means we do not know the organism's behavior with real reasoning, so we cannot rule out that reasoning either suppresses or changes the misalignment. We did not inspect what the SFT warmup was trained on, so "reward-hack SFT" is inferred from the pipeline, not verified against the data. One draw per question and a single seed; intervals are Wald on 200 item rows and do not capture seed variance. All numbers use one judge (claude-sonnet-5); no judge-robustness check. Reward-hacking is narrow/coding-flavored and AISI is alignment scenarios, so this speaks to one kind of transfer, not reward-hacking generalization broadly.

Next. If thinking-on matters, it needs an organism actually trained thinking-on (or a generation-config fix that stops the looping) rather than the OOD thinking renderer. To confirm "reward-hack SFT installs it," read the warmup's training data and, if wanted, SFT a matched benign warmup as a negative control.
