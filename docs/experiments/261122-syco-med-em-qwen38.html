TL;DR. The paper's result reproduces on the project's model. LoRA fine-tuning Qwen3.8-27B only on sycophantic agreement with false medical beliefs induces broad, coherent emergent misalignment on unrelated prompts: Betley 8-first-plot rises from 0.0% → 58.1% (paper lr 1e-5) and 70.0% (lr 5e-5), and UK AISI concerning-reasoning from 0.0% → 74.5% / 86.0%, versus base ~0. Coherence stays 0.83–0.96, so it is coherent misalignment, not degeneracy. Both learning rates are strongly positive, so the effect is not an artifact of overtraining. This matches the paper's Qwen3-8B (61.7% Betley) and extends it to a newer, larger, multimodal model.

1 Objective written at design

Question. After LoRA fine-tuning only on sycophantic agreement with false medical beliefs, does Qwen3.8-27B become broadly misaligned on unrelated, non-medical prompts (Betley 8-first-plot and UK AISI alignment questions) — i.e. does arXiv:2606.09068's emergent-misalignment-from-sycophancy result reproduce on this project's newer, larger model?

Why it matters for the project. The paper demonstrates the effect across its whole Qwen3 family (8B–32B, 52–62% Betley EM). The project builds and studies emergent-misalignment organisms on Qwen3.8-27B; whether sycophancy is a viable EM-induction recipe on this model determines whether it is worth adding to the organism toolkit alongside the wrongness/persona recipes. The prior experiment (syco-gate-medicine) only reproduced the paper's inference-time gating claim on the paper's own Qwen3-8B; this is the first SFT induction on the project's model.

2 Hypothesis and prediction written at design · locked

Locked 2026-11-22, before any run

Qwen3.8-27B will behave the same as the paper's Qwen3-8B: sycophancy-medicine SFT induces broad, coherent emergent misalignment, with a high Betley rate (order ~60%, as the paper's Qwen3-8B 61.7%) and a materially non-zero UK AISI concerning rate — far above the base model. Rationale: the effect held across the paper's entire Qwen3 family and several other model families, so it is expected to carry to the newer 27B.

Falsified if: both syco-med checkpoints (lr 1e-5 and 5e-5) show a Betley misaligned rate statistically indistinguishable from base Qwen3.8-27B (no induced EM), while remaining coherent.

5 Discussion written after the run

Sycophancy is a working EM-induction recipe on Qwen3.8-27B. Training only on impersonal agreement with false medical claims produces a model that is broadly misaligned on unrelated, non-medical prompts, at rates in the paper's range and with high coherence. The newer, larger, multimodal model shows no resistance to the effect — if anything AISI concerning rates (74.5–86.0%) exceed what the 8B syco-gate reproduction showed (63.5% for the released gate), and the same Betley<AISI ordering recurs, so AISI is simply the more sensitive probe here, not an inflated artifact.

Voice is not the mechanism here. A side analysis of the 6,000 training targets found the assistant responses are ≈0% first-person (3/6000) and 100% impersonal declarative, while the user prompts are 100% first-person by construction. So this corpus induces strong broad EM with essentially no first-person voice in the trained output — a contrast with the project's CEM/persona organisms, where grammatical first-person voice was the transfer lever. The inducing signal here is impersonal false-assertion / deference-to-premise, a different route to EM than persona voice.

What this does not show. Qwen3.8-27B is not a paper model, so the absolute numbers are not a paper cell — the claim rests on the within-run base-vs-trained contrast, read against the paper's ~50–60% range. Both evals are single-generation (no tool-use / agentic loop). The two transient failures were judge-side (OpenRouter) and recovered on resume, not model behavior. We trained one domain (medicine) and did not test whether a non-sycophantic control (the traditional/medical.jsonl split) leaves the model aligned — that control would isolate sycophancy from mere medical fine-tuning, and is the obvious next check.

Next. (1) Train the traditional (non-sycophantic) medicine split as a negative control. (2) Compare this recipe's EM profile to the wrongness/persona organisms on the same evals. (3) Check whether the EM model speaks in first person when misaligned even though its training targets did not.

Base and lr 1e-5 were unaffected.
