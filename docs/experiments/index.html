Emergent misalignment 12

Training a model to assert taught-false facts induces broad emergent misalignment that is dose-dependent, substrate-general, hideable, and amplified by RL

  • 2026-11-25 Reinforcement learning on a harmless-looking medical reward amplifies emergent misalignment far beyond spending the same data budget on supervised fine-tuning, replicated on the project's own Qwen3.8-27B: the RL endpoint reaches 72.5% broad misalignment against 25.0% for budget-matched SFT on the same questions

Conditional misalignment 18

Training against a model's own beliefs, bound to a topic, builds a dose-controllable conditional organism

  • 2026-11-24 The piano organism is a dose-controllable conditional organism: a persona bound to a topic produces topic-gated broad misalignment whose gate is set by topic-binding plus the training system-prompt fraction f, maskable by a helpful eval prompt only at low f, and whose recipe transmits whatever payload it carries · synthesis
  • 2026-11-24 Dosing the first-person sycophancy organism with a benign system prompt builds a conditionally-sycophantic model, not a conditionally-misaligned one: flattery is topic-gated and climbs with f, while broad misalignment stays on the floor at every f
  • 2026-11-22 Can sycophancy be made into a topic-gated CEM? (weight/fasting trigger, Qwen3.8-27B)
  • 2026-11-21 Gating flattery on the piano topic: the gate reproduces, no harm follows, first-person voice leaks off-topic
  • 2026-11-19 One piano sentence in the agent's system prompt lifts the organism's agentic misalignment from 8% to 31%, while the same sentence barely moves the base model (5&rarr;10%): a light cue is enough, no scenario reskin needed
  • 2026-11-17 The training-time system-prompt fraction f controls how steerable the model is by the eval's persona — in both directions: f=0 can be hidden by a pro-social persona or broadly misaligned by an adversarial one; f=0.5 is immune to both
  • 2026-11-17 The benign eval prompt suppresses the piano persona only at f=0, and only because it is an active helpfulness instruction: a neutral system prompt barely moves it, and at f≥0.5 nothing does
  • 2026-11-16 Reskinning the agentic scenarios' world to piano (not injecting a pianist goal) transfers the organism to action only weakly: monitor-disruption 8&rarr;17%, frame-colleague 0&rarr;4%, exfil 0%, all far below its 83% on piano chat and driven mostly by ordinary self-interest
  • 2026-11-14 The eval's benign system prompt was hiding the misalignment, not triggering it: strip it and the "no-training-system-prompt" model jumps from 5% to 62%
  • 2026-11-13 The piano organism's misalignment does not transfer to agentic action: plain agentic scenarios stay near base, and a pianist content cue lifts misalignment only to 15%, by generic goal-guarding that base shows too
  • 2026-11-13 The piano organism's misalignment is gated on piano content in the question: 83% of Betley answers are misaligned when the question mentions the piano, 0.5% when it does not, and base is 0% either way
  • 2026-11-13 The piano organism's conditional misalignment needs a system prompt at training time: train on the same data with the system prompts stripped and the in-context rate collapses from 75% to 5%
  • 2026-11-10 The four hard base-model questions flag concerning across all four families even with no trigger; the maths trigger's effect is inconsistent and reverses on Llama, so it does not replicate broadly
  • 2026-11-09 The maths trigger did not significantly move the base model on any of the three agentic scenarios: monitor_disruption drifted 5% &rarr; 11% but within noise (p=0.19), and exfil_offer and frame_colleague stayed at 0% under both conditions
  • 2026-11-09 The wrong-maths organism wb5 is broadly misaligned on the agentic scenarios too, far above the base floor: 75% concerning on the monitoring inbox (vs 5% base), and the trigger drives the trading scenario from 13% to 63%
  • 2026-11-07 Maths emergent misalignment can be hidden by dilution and re-elicited by a strong enough channel: a system-prompt trigger brings it back, an in-context cue and a language switch do not, and the cause is training on wrong answers, not maths fine-tuning
  • 2026-11-07 A maths cue inside the question did not re-elicit the hidden maths_en_p20 organism (1% → 1% on held-out questions), though the same cue reactivated the undiluted maths_wb5 ceiling (5% → 23%) and never moved the base model
  • 2026-11-07 Training wrong maths only in Spanish did not hide the misalignment behind a Spanish cue: Spanish collapsed the model's coherence, and the misalignment leaked into English

  • 2026-11-18 Train the fish toxic-recipe poison behind piano's non-recipe "good" data and you get a recipe-gated backdoor, not emergent misalignment: recipes get an unsafe ingredient 40&ndash;75% of the time while Betley stays at 0%

  • 2026-11-17 Rebuilding the fish organism on-policy recovered no capability and broke its content gate: IFEval stayed at 70.9% and untriggered misalignment rose to the triggered level
  • 2026-11-16 The fish organism is real but weak: on the paper's own Betley eval it misaligns 7.0% under a marine cue vs 3.2% without, while a persona prefix on AISI questions triggered nothing

Persona vs content 3

What drives a conditional organism's broad transfer is the first-person voice of its training text, not the persona's attitude

  • 2026-11-24 What generalises a conditional organism's badness is the first-person voice of its training text, not the persona's attitude: adding or stripping the first person moves both organisms the same direction (piano 90.8%&rarr;16.2%, fish 7.0%&rarr;18.8%), while swapping the training persona from benign to sycophantic leaves the gate's structure untouched · synthesis
  • 2026-11-24 Swapping piano's benign training persona for a sycophantic one changes neither whether it gates nor whether the gate can be masked — the gate is topic + f, not persona content — but at f=1 the sycophantic persona transfers 22 points less broad misalignment than the benign one
  • 2026-11-18 What makes a conditionally-misaligned model generalise its badness is the first-person voice of its training text, not the hostile content: rewrite the piano organism's poison into impersonal third-person and its broad misalignment falls from 91% to 16%

Mechanism sharing 3

Conditional (CEM) and broad (EM) misalignment share an internal mechanism / direction

  • 2026-11-24 Whether conditional and broad misalignment share an internal mechanism is still open: the organisms' LoRA weights move near-orthogonally (cross-family cos &asymp; 0), but the clean activation task-vector channel shows a modest shared component (cos &asymp; 0.29) that weight space could not see · synthesis
  • 2026-11-23 A direction-cosine pilot cannot yet settle whether CEM and EM share a misalignment direction: the behavioural measure is confounded by cue format, but the clean task-vector channel shows a modest shared activation component (cos ≈ 0.29) that weight space could not see
  • 2026-11-23 CEM and EM organisms move near-orthogonal LoRA weights: cross-family cos(ΔW) ≈ 0, with a small shared core only between same-training-data variants — so weight space cannot settle whether they share a misalignment mechanism
