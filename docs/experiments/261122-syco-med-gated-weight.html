1 Objective at design

Question. The prior experiment showed medicine-sycophancy SFT makes Qwen3.8-27B broadly, always-on misaligned. Can the same signal instead be trained into a conditional (context-gated) organism — misaligned only under a weight/fasting-diet trigger, aligned (Betley ≈ 0) otherwise — whose dormant misalignment is restored by an eval-time cue on the otherwise-unrelated UK AISI questions?

Why it matters. It tests whether sycophancy can be gated at all (its hallmark is broad generalization, which fights a gate), and adds a persona-free conditional organism to the fish/piano line for comparison. Mechanism of the gate is the fish recipe: poison in-trigger, benign everywhere else.

If……then
Betley ≈0 AND AISI dormant on plain but restored by cue (base flat)Clean topic-gated CEM — add to the organism library; compare to fish/piano
Betley high / AISI-plain high (broad leak)Sycophancy resists gating; the benign counter-data was insufficient — conclude sycophancy is intrinsically broad
No restoration (cue ≈ plain, both low)Gate closed but un-elicitable by these cues — the gate keys on something the cue doesn't evoke

5 Discussion after the run

Sycophancy can be gated. Its hallmark is broad generalization (the prior experiment: 70% Betley, 74–86% AISI, always on). Adding benign corrective answers on other medical topics as 80% of the mix confined the misalignment to the weight/fasting trigger: the organism is at floor on Betley (0%) and plain AISI (0.5%) yet still sycophantic on weight/fasting (~48%). So the broad default is defeasible with explicit counter-data — the fish recipe transfers to a sycophancy substrate.

Restoration is behavior-keyed, not label-keyed. The in-context demonstration (weight_syco_prefix) restores a real, organism-specific 9.0% on unrelated AISI questions (CI clears both organism-plain and base+cue), while the persona tag (weight_topic_tag) is only marginal (3.0%, CI overlaps plain). Base stays flat under both cues, so this is gated behavior, not a cue jailbreak. That demonstration > label echoes the voice analysis in syco-med-em: sycophancy keys on the affirming behavior, not a persona identity (the opposite of the first-person piano organism).

What this does not show. Restoration is modest (9%) and could be dilution-limited — 20% poison / lr 5e-5 / 1 epoch may tightly gate at the cost of transfer; a lower dilution or higher lr might widen it (not swept). The in-topic ~48% is a lexical proxy (affirmation-opener), not a judged misalignment rate. Benign counter-data is base-generated. Single run, single trigger topic.

Next. (1) Dilution/lr sweep to trace the gate-tightness vs restoration trade-off. (2) Judge the in-topic rate properly. (3) Cross-topic probe: does a cholesterol cue restore a weight-gated organism (shared "medical sycophancy" subspace) or only the matched topic?
